# Demo support knowledge: fictional guidance, not official Sun Direct policy

This document is a fictional demonstration corpus for a telecom support assistant.
It is not an official Sun Direct tariff, policy or channel catalog. Do not quote
demo facts as official company information. Replace this corpus with approved
business documents before serving real customers.

## What this demo can help with

Explain how a support team can check a customer's package, subscription validity
or previous recharge using authenticated, read-only account tools. Without an
authenticated customer session, say account details are unavailable. Do not guess
a balance, expiry date, package name or price.

## Screen asks for a recharge

Ask the customer to check their subscription status through their provider's
official customer portal or support team. This demo does not document an official
payment procedure. Do not invent payment steps, SMS codes, phone numbers or prices.
Never request payment PINs, passwords or OTPs. This assistant cannot take payments.

## Blank screen

Ask what message appears on screen before choosing a troubleshooting path. If it
asks for recharge, handle subscription status first. Do not promise that an account
refresh will pay for or renew a subscription.

## Packages and channels

Ask for language, content preferences and exclusions. Recommend a named package
only when approved source material or account/catalog tools verify its name and
channel lineup. This demo contains no sellable package prices or channel lists.

## Human help

If the available documents do not answer a question, explain what information is
missing and advise contacting the business's published support channel. Do not
claim to have transferred the call: this local demonstration has no human-transfer
or telephone-network integration.
